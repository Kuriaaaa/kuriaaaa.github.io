## The brief

Brimotors Limited needed more than a brochure site: a place to sell cars online and a back office to run the showroom.

## What I built

**For buyers:** stock search, car pages with photos and video, PDF pro-forma invoices, offers, waiting lists, price-drop alerts, test-drive booking, trade-ins, financing requests and M-Pesa deposits to reserve a car.

**For staff:** a back office for vehicles, leads, offers, testimonials and staff roles — with an audit log of every change.

**For documents:** a vault for logbooks and IDs. Every file is virus-scanned, encrypted, watermarked on view and shared through one-time links.

## Result

Lighthouse scores of 100 for accessibility and 91–97 for speed on mobile 4G across public pages. Automated tests, security scanning and nightly off-site backups run on every change.
