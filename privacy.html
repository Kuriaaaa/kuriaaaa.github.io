Privacy and data protection

Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Chege John Brian Kuria handles information submitted through this portfolio and optional website analytics. It is written with the principles of Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 in mind.

Effective date: 22 August 2026 · Next review: August 2027

1. Data controller

Chege John Brian Kuria determines why and how information submitted through this personal portfolio is used. Contact through the portfolio enquiry form, at johnkuria6996@gmail.com, or by telephone on +254 717 246 699.

2. Information collected

  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, optional organization, enquiry type and message.
  • Consent preference: a browser-local record of whether analytics was accepted.
  • Optional analytics: page interactions, clicks, scrolling, device/browser characteristics and approximate location. Contact-form fields are masked.

No payment, national identification, health or account-password information is requested.

3. Purpose and lawful basis

Enquiry information is used only to assess and reply to employment, internship, freelance, collaboration or project-review requests. Processing is necessary to respond to the visitor’s request and for legitimate professional communication. The required form checkbox records express agreement to transmission through FormSubmit, including possible processing outside Kenya. Optional analytics is used only after affirmative consent to understand site usability and improve the portfolio. Analytics consent can be withdrawn through .

4. Service providers and transfers

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Enquiries are transmitted through FormSubmit to the portfolio email account. When analytics consent is granted, Microsoft Clarity processes interaction data for heatmaps and session analysis. These providers may process information outside Kenya. Their current safeguards and policies apply alongside this notice.

  • GitHub privacy statement
  • FormSubmit privacy policy
  • Microsoft privacy statement

5. Retention

Portfolio enquiries are normally retained for no longer than 12 months after the last relevant communication, unless an active opportunity or legal obligation requires longer retention. Microsoft Clarity retains playback data for 30 days and heatmap/click data for up to 9 months under its current service policy. The browser-local consent preference remains until it is changed or browser storage is cleared.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable Kenyan law, you may ask to be informed about processing, access your information, object to processing, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of information that is no longer required, or withdraw analytics consent. Email the address above with the subject “Privacy request”. You may also raise a complaint with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

7. Security

Controls include HTTPS hosting, a restrictive content-security policy, masked form fields in analytics, anti-spam fields, consent-gated analytics and exclusion of private project documents and credentials from the public repository. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Questions and changes

Privacy enquiries: use the portfolio enquiry form or write to johnkuria6996@gmail.com.
Telephone: +254 717 246 699

This notice should be reviewed whenever the form, analytics provider, hosting service or processing purpose changes, and at least annually.
